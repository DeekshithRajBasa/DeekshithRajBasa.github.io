01 — Logo

Pointer D + drb

A rounded capital D whose counter is a mouse pointer — a letter first, an interface second. Paired with a lowercase drb wordmark built from one ring-and-stem system.

↔

Clear space

Keep free space around the logo equal to the pointer's width on every side.

◎

Minimum size

Symbol: 16 px (favicon). Lockup: 24 px tall. Below 24 px the pointer becomes a notch — that's expected.

✕

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or outline the mark
  • Recolour outside the brand palette
  • Add shadows or effects to the symbol
  • Place the blue mark on busy or blue backgrounds — use white
